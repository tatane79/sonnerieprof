Support

How can we help?

Find answers to common questions and a way to contact support.

Contact support

For questions, suggestions or bug reports, email sonnerieprof.app@proton.me.

For a bug report: if possible, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Sonnerie Prof version and a screenshot.

The wrong school site is selected

You can always select a site manually. Location access is optional and is only used to help select the nearest configured site.

The widget looks out of date

Open Sonnerie Prof and check that the correct school site and bell times are configured. iOS controls part of the refresh cadence for widgets and Lock Screen content.

How do I edit bell times?

Open Sonnerie Prof Settings, choose the relevant site and then the day you want to edit. Changes are saved automatically.

Are tips required?

No. Sonnerie Prof is fully functional for free. Tips only support development and never unlock features.
