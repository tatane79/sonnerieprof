Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sonnerie Prof is designed to help teachers track the time remaining until the next school bell.

User accounts

No account is required to use Sonnerie Prof.

Data stored on the device

School sites, bell times, timetables and preferences are stored locally on the device. Some configuration data is shared locally between the app and its widget using an iOS App Group.

Location

Location access is optional. When allowed, it is used only to determine which configured school site is closest so the app can select the appropriate schedule. Location is not sent to the developer and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Optional tips

Sonnerie Prof offers optional consumable In-App Purchases that let users support development. No purchase unlocks any feature. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit; the developer does not receive your payment card details.

The app stores a local flag after a successful tip so automatic support reminders stop on that installation.

Advertising and tracking

Sonnerie Prof contains no advertising SDK and does not perform cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Analytics and servers

The current version of Sonnerie Prof contains no third-party analytics service and does not send school sites, schedules or location to a server operated by the developer.

Apple services

The app uses Apple system technologies such as Core Location, WidgetKit, ActivityKit and StoreKit. These services are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy or support questions: sonnerieprof.app@proton.me
